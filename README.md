EMBERFALL — a top-down arena survival shooter with a goal: survive 10 waves and slay the Ember King. You are an ember wisp in a dark arena. Dodge Cinders, Spitters, Brutes, zigzagging Darts and long-range Snipers, beat the Cinderlord every 5th wave, and face the Ember King on wave 10 — kill him and the dawn breaks.

After every cleared wave you draft an ember: pick 1 of 3 permanent upgrades (fire rate, bullet damage, piercing shots, speed, magnet, health, dash, aegis shield) and build your wisp your way. Chain spark pickups for a combo up to ×8, chase kill streaks (RAMPAGE! INFERNO! EMBERSTORM!), and grab hearts, triple flame and shields. Embered elites — gold-ringed, double health, double score — stalk the later waves.

WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot, Shift to dash. Synthesized WebAudio sound with a music loop that heats up on boss waves, particles, screen shake, muzzle flash, damage numbers, and a best score that persists.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080
